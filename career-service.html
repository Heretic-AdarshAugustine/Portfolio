The Problem

Student

Starting a new course is an overwhelming experience for many students, especially students coming from diverse educational and work backgrounds.

Students often question how their talents and training fit into career pathways, often without a clear sense of direction.

Without proper guidance, the transition from education to employment can feel disorienting and discouraging.

Students need support to evaluate their skills, understand their potential in the industry, and connect with career pathways that match their interests and strengths.

Research Focus & Objectives

How might we help first-year students gain clarity, confidence, and direction when navigating career pathways and support services?

Objectives

Identify gaps in current career services

Assess services utilization and understand barriers

Understand student expectations and needs

Reccommend strategies to enhance career support

Survey Findings

To gather quantitative insights from our target demographic we recruited over 100 participants using strategies such as cold approaches, snowball sampling, and social media advertising. By agreeing to the informed consent, the survey began with a screening question to identify individuals within our target demographic.

Underutilization of services

Most of the other services were underutilized suggesting either low awareness or misalignment with student needs.

Interview Findings

From the survey respondents, students who had shown their interest in further participation in the study were invited for an interview. These semi-structured interviews helped us gather insights into the experiences and challenges students face while seeking career services. The informed consent was sent to the participants before the interview to decide their participation in the study.

Usability issues with the college career platform

Students face challenges with platform’s usability, including outdated listings and a non-user friendly interface.

“The job portal is confusing and outdated. It’s hard to find relevant opportunities.”

Time management and academic balance

Balancing academics with career development activities is a major challenge for many students.

“It’s hard to attend workshops or events when I have back-to-back classes and assignments.”

Desire for more personalized and industry-aligned support

Students want tailored guidance that aligns with their career goals and industry trends.

“I wish I could get advice that’s more specific to my major and the industry I want to enter.”

Strategic Opportunities

Based on the survey and interview findings, three strategic opportunities emerged to reshape how career services reach and support first-year students.

Career Dashboard

One intuitive platform for all career resources — job postings, mentorship, workshops, events and career tools.

Program specific Guidance

Tailored career pathways, program specific resources, and alumni success stories to show real possibilities.

Mentorship hub

A platform dedicated to connecting with alumni, faculty, and industry professionals for guidance and support.

Outcome

This research identifies three strategic opportunities to improve career support for first year students by making services:

  • Easier to discover
  • More personalized
  • Better integrated
  • More aligned with student needs
